# AISA·AI - Comparative Advantage Engine for Economic Diversification

## Overview

AISA·AI is a professional web-based tool that identifies **Revealed Comparative Advantage (RCA)** and **Latent Comparative Advantage** sectors for countries based on their factor endowments, following the **New Structural Economics** framework developed by **Professor Justin Yifu Lin** (former World Bank Chief Economist).

The tool replicates and extends the methodology of **Dr. Farai Chigaru's UNDP award-winning software**, automating the identification of **Comparative Advantage Following (CAF)** sectors to guide economic diversification and industrial policy.

## Features

- **Balassa RCA Index Calculation** - Industry-standard revealed comparative advantage methodology
- **25+ Sector Coverage** - Textiles, electronics, machinery, pharmaceuticals, ICT, and more
- **Factor Endowment Analysis** - Labor, capital, and resource intensity weighting
- **Benchmark Country Identification** - Lin's 100-300% GDP per capita rule
- **GIFF 6-Step Policy Lab** - Actionable industrial policy recommendations
- **PDF Export** - Professional policy reports for policymakers
- **Global Database** - 15+ major economies with full data
- **Responsive Design** - Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile

## Methodology

### New Structural Economics (Lin, 2011)

- Economic structure is endogenous to factor endowments
- Countries should follow comparative advantage (CAF strategy)
- Government plays facilitating role in structural transformation
